About Figment: Curating Heritage, Crafting Experiences

Figment transforms Singapore's iconic heritage shophouses into stylish, fully-furnished coliving homes, primarily for expatriates and working professionals. Our mission is to preserve Singapore's architectural soul while delivering strong returns and exceptional living experiences.

Understanding the Market: Singapore Real Estate & Coliving

The Singapore real estate market remains a key destination for global capital, with heritage shophouses representing a scarce and highly sought-after asset class. These properties offer capital preservation potential and a unique cultural appeal. Figment's specialized coliving operations are designed to significantly enhance rental yields and asset value compared to traditional leasing, capitalizing on evolving demographics, lifestyle preferences for community and flexibility, and housing affordability challenges.

The Figment Advantage & Seed Portfolio

Seed Portfolio & Operational Expertise

Figment brings deep expertise in sourcing, restoring, and operating heritage shophouses for coliving and other potential uses in Singapore. Our operational model, refined across over 40 properties, consistently enhances asset value and tenant satisfaction.

  • The Fund will be seeded with a portfolio of Figment's existing, stabilized assets, ensuring immediate capital deployment and reducing initial lease-up risks.
  • Seed assets to be acquired at an attractive discount to current independent valuations, offering potential immediate equity upside.
  • Valuation by a reputable, independent third-party valuer to ensure transparency and fairness.
  • Focus on prime and strategic Singapore shophouse locations.

Value Creation through Aggregation

Figment's strategy includes creating an aggregation premium by consolidating a portfolio of scattered-site shophouses from fragmented ownership. This builds an institutional-scale collection, enhancing liquidity and attractiveness for a potential portfolio sale.
